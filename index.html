I am a fourth year PhD student at the University of Washington working in the Natural Language Processing Group and the Machine Learning Group. I am advised by Professor Yejin Choi. I am also currently a part-time researcher on Project Alexandria at the Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence and on the Deep Learning team at Microsoft Research Redmond. My research centers on architecting deep learning models that learn to simulate common sense reasoning and use the resulting representations for generation and dialogue tasks.
