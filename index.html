Biography

I am an assistant professor in the School of Computer and Communication Sciences at EPFL. I lead the EPFL NLP group where we conduct research on natural language processing (NLP) systems that can model, represent, and reason about human and world knowledge.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Stanford University working in the SNAP and NLP groups working with Jure Leskovec and Chris Manning. I completed my PhD in NLP at the University of Washington, where I worked with Yejin Choi.

If you are interesting in joining EPFL NLP, please read the following guide.

Joining EPFL NLP

I receive many e-mails about joining (or completing projects with) EPFL NLP. I wish I could accommodate every request, but I cannot respond to each of them individually.

If you’re interested in joining the EPFL NLP group, please read the following:

I am…

Looking for a postdoc position – Congratulations on receiving a PhD! I will be hiring a postdoc to begin around Fall 2022 (can be moderately sooner / later depending on your timeline). If you’re interested, e-mail me with a CV and research statement.

An EDIC fellow – I’m happy to supervise rotations provided our research interests align and there’s a good chance that the rotation will lead to a permanent position in the lab.

An EDIC admissible student – If you think your background and interests are a good fit for the research conducted by the EPFL NLP lab, please e-mail me.

An EPFL master’s student – I’m happy to supervise master’s projects every semester. If you’re interested in doing yours with EPFL NLP, shoot me an e-mail. Please attach your CV and transcript.

Applying to the EPFL EDIC PhD program – Great to hear you’re applying to EPFL! Before you can be considered for the NLP lab, you will have to be admitted to the EDIC program, which handles admissions. Feel free to reach out and let me know if you apply (or if you intend to apply), but I unfortunately can’t conduct pre-screenings until applications are in.

Looking for an internship: Please apply through the Summer@EPFL program.
